# Contract Risk Taxonomy

High severity risk:

- Auto-renewal with notice window that can be missed.
- Unilateral price change, unilateral service suspension, unilateral amendment.
- Broad indemnity or uncapped liability.
- High contractual penalty or liquidated damages without clear cap.
- Personal data breach, cross-border data transfer without clear safeguards.
- Missing or weak confidentiality, data processing or security obligations.
- Overdue compliance document that already passed its deadline.
- Non-compete or non-solicit clauses that restrict hiring or operations.

Medium severity risk:

- Conflicting commercial values across attachments.
- Ambiguous OCR date or unclear deadline.
- Governing law/jurisdiction mismatch.
- Deemed acceptance after silence.
- Audit cost reimbursement without clear limitation.
- Payment penalties with cap.

Low severity risk:

- Standard reporting obligation.
- Routine notice address.
- Minor formatting or missing optional metadata.

Risk output rules:

- Severity must be justified in detail.
- Each risk must include source, page_number and recommendation.
- High risks should normally create review_queue items and task candidates.
